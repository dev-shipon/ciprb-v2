WHO WE ARE

Leading Public Health Research & Implementation

Centre for Injury Prevention and Research, Bangladesh (CIPRB) is a premier non-profit institute dedicated to saving lives and reducing preventable deaths and disabilities. The landmark Bangladesh Health and Injury Survey (BHIS 2005), the largest community-based injury survey conducted in any low- and middle-income country, revealed the staggering burden of injuries as a major killer of children.

"The magnitude of injury explored steered researchers towards an institutional response, establishing CIPRB on 6 February 2005 to pioneer community-level safety and public health transformation."
  • PRECISE Trial: The world's largest community trial on child injury prevention.
  • Anchal Model: Community crèches proven to reduce drowning risk by over 80%.
  • SwimSafe: Over 700,000 rural graduates recognized by the World Health Organization.

CASE STORY & HUMAN IMPACT

Sabitri Robidas: From Despair to Hope

1. Early Life & Tragic Turning Point

Married at an early age without access to emergency obstetric care in tea garden estates, Sabitri endured days of obstructed labor that led to the devastating loss of her unborn baby and left her with an obstetric fistula.

2. Years of Silent Suffering

Stigmatized and unable to perform daily tasks without leakages, she faced total isolation from community gatherings and loss of livelihood for over two agonizing years.

3. Medical Intervention & Complete Healing

Identified by CIPRB community field workers, Sabitri was enrolled in specialized reconstructive surgery at a tertiary hospital, receiving comprehensive post-operative care and fully healing.

4. Rebuilding Independence with Sewing Machine

CIPRB provided Sabitri with vocational tailoring training and an official sewing machine, empowering her to earn a respectable income and reintegrate with pride into her community.
