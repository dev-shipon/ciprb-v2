## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the rigor of an evidence-based global health research institute combined with the compassionate urgency of humanitarian fieldwork. The visual aesthetic fuses Modern Corporate clarity with Human-Centered Research sensibilities. It speaks to an international audience of epidemiologists, donor agencies (such as WHO, UNICEF, Bloomberg Philanthropies), policymakers, and grassroots community workers across low- and middle-income countries.

The emotional tone balances institutional trustworthiness, scientific precision, and urgent humanitarian empathy. Every screen communicates clinical credibility without sterility, grounding research data in life-saving reality. The interface relies on crisp white fields, crystalline aquatic tones recalling water safety initiatives, deep institutional navy for authoritative anchoring, and energetic accents representing emergency prevention and active community health programs.

## Colors

The color system is rooted in public health research, water safety, and injury prevention:
- **Primary (`#004B93`)**: Institutional research navy. Used for main brand identifiers, primary buttons, major navigation surfaces, and high-hierarchy headings.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7`)**: Vivid ocean azure. Directly honors aquatic life-saving programs (like SwimSafe), providing an approachable, energetic interactive tone for secondary actions, interactive states, progress meters, and active tabs.
- **Tertiary / Emergency Coral (`#E0533C`)**: Emergency intervention accent. Derived from vital warning, burn prevention, and life-saving case stories. Used sparingly for critical badges, urgent callouts, and key conversion moments.
- **Neutral (`#0B1D3A`)**: Deep ink slate. Replaces stark pure black to establish higher typographic refinement, optimal contrast ratios (AAA compliance on light backgrounds), and grounded legibility.
- **Accent Gold (`#F59E0B`)**: Humanitarian warmth. Applied in statistical counters, honorary award highlights, and contextual indicators.
- **Surface Ice (`#F0F5FA`)**: A cool, clean secondary background wash that separates programmatic modules from pure white canvas sections without introducing visual noise.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** with **Open Sans**.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers precise geometry and warmth to titles, quantitative metrics, and labels. Its balanced terminals and open apertures provide distinction to programmatic innovations (e.g., SwimSafe, Anchal, Drowning Prevention) without appearing clinical or detached.
- **Open Sans** provides optimal legibility for extensive epidemiological data, research reports, field stories, and case summaries. Its tall x-height and open forms ensure effortless reading across screens of varying quality and resolution.
- Section tags, category pills, and micro-headers rely on `label-caps` in uppercase styling with deliberate 0.08em tracking to organize complex programmatic verticals cleanly.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid, anchoring institutional density on desktop and vertical clarity on touch screens:

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12 columns with 1.5rem gutters and max-width containers capped at 1280px to preserve data density and reading comfort.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with 1.25rem gutters and 1.5rem safe margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 1rem gutters and 1rem edge margins.

Vertical rhythm relies on multiples of 8px. Impact statistical strips collapse into a 2x2 grid on mobile while spanning 4 balanced columns on desktop. Program card decks transition from a 3-column overview on desktop to horizontal swipe rails or single-column stacks on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth follows a medical and scientific clarity model: flat surfaces, pristine contrast, and soft ambient illumination rather than heavy skeuomorphic shadows.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Flat `#FFFFFF` or `#F0F5FA` tint. No elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Units)**: Subtle 1px solid border (`#D8E2EE`) paired with an ambient diffuse shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(11, 29, 58, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Floating Elements)**: Elevated state upon user engagement: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(0, 75, 147, 0.10)`. The shadow subtly carries the primary navy hue, reinforcing optical clarity.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, and Drawer Navigation)**: Deep spatial isolation: `0 20px 38px -6px rgba(11, 29, 58, 0.18)` over a 40% opacity ink scrim.

## Shapes

The design uses **Rounded** corner tokens (Radius Level 2). Standard interface components use an 8px (`0.5rem`) base radius, while larger hero containers, featured program cards, and promotional callouts leverage `1rem` (`rounded-lg`). 

Category indicators, pill-tags, search badges, and key thematic filters adopt full pill rounding (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical metadata from action-oriented rectangular surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid institutional navy (`#004B93`) fill with white text, font weight 600, 8px corner radius, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. On hover: shifts to `#003B75` with a subtle +2px upward transform.
- **Secondary Action Button**: Vivid ocean azure (`#0284C7`) background with white text, for immediate action paths (e.g., "View All Innovations").
- **Outline / Ghost Button**: White background, 1.5px border (`#004B93`), `#004B93` label. Hover transforms to a faint ice blue fill (`#F0F5FA`).
- **Emergency / Donate Action**: Emergent coral (`#E0533C`) background, reserved strictly for urgent donations or high-priority humanitarian interventions.

### Cards
- **Program & Innovation Cards**: White backdrop with an 8px radius, clipped overflow for top imagery, a 1px border (`#D8E2EE`), and a 20px content interior. Include an integrated metadata pill (e.g., "Child Safety", "Drowning Prevention") positioned over the media header.
- **Case Story & Narrative Cards**: Warm card variations, occasionally featuring an emergency coral header stripe or an ambient gold bookmark to signify human impact testimonials.

### Impact Stat Counters
- Dark ink slate (`#0B1D3A`) or institutional navy (`#004B93`) solid background cards.
- Numerical values set in `stat-counter` typography rendered in vibrant azure (`#0284C7`) or accent gold (`#F59E0B`).
- Accompanying descriptions set in uppercase 11px Jakarta Sans (`#FFFFFF` with 80% opacity) for high legibility and field validation.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Active State**: Solid `#004B93` background, pure white text, fully rounded.
- **Inactive State**: `#F0F5FA` background, `#53647E` text, subtle 1px border (`#D8E2EE`).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- Text inputs use a crisp white background with a 1px `#D8E2EE` border, 8px radius, and 44px minimum touch target.
- Focused states feature a 2px outer ring in ocean azure (`#0284C7`) with zero border color bleeding.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom styled 18px rounded controls using primary navy (`#004B93`) fill upon selection and crisp white indicator iconography.
